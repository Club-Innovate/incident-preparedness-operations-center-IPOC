# Legal Escalation Matrix

## Purpose

Define legal/compliance escalation paths for potential breach scenarios.

## Escalation Matrix

| Severity | Scenario | Primary Owner | Escalate To | Target Escalation Time | Notification Decision Authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SEV-1 | Confirmed or highly likely PHI exposure / active compromise | Incident Commander | Compliance Lead + Legal Counsel + Executive Sponsor | <= 4 hours | Legal Counsel + Compliance Lead |
| SEV-2 | Potential PHI exposure with incomplete evidence | Compliance Lead | Legal Counsel + Security Engineering Lead | <= 1 business day | Legal Counsel + Compliance Lead |
| SEV-3 | Low confidence event / no confirmed exposure | Security Engineering Lead | Compliance Lead | <= 2 business days | Compliance Lead |

## Contact Validation Cadence

- Verify escalation contacts quarterly.
- Record validation results in incident response tabletop notes.
